# prosnooz support

prosnooz is an iPhone app that records your night and scores your snoring. This repository is where you can report problems and send feedback.

- **Report a bug or request a feature:** [open an issue](https://github.com/scottham/prosnooz-issues/issues/new)
- **Privacy policy:** [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md)

## When reporting a problem

Please include:

1. iPhone model and iOS version
2. App version (shown in the App Store listing)
3. What you did, what you expected, and what happened
4. A screenshot if it helps

Do not attach recordings that you would not want to be public. Issues here are visible to everyone.

## Common questions

**Do I need to keep the screen on?** No. Start recording, lock the phone and leave it by your pillow.

**Will calls and alarms still work?** Yes. A call pauses the recording and it resumes afterwards.

**Where is my data?** On your iPhone. iCloud backup is optional and uses your own iCloud Drive.

**Is this a medical app?** No. It does not diagnose sleep apnea or any other condition.
