# prosnooz Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

prosnooz is an iPhone app by prokids that records sound overnight and analyzes snoring. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

## Summary

- The developer does not collect any data from you.
- The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.
- Recordings and results stay on your iPhone, unless you choose to back them up to your own iCloud or export them yourself.

## Data the app handles

**Microphone audio.** With your permission, the app records audio while a recording session is running. Audio is analyzed on your device to detect snoring. It is never sent to the developer or to any server operated by the developer.

**Results and notes.** Snore scores, timelines, the factors you tag and the notes you write are stored on your device.

**Battery level.** The app reads the battery level at the start and end of a session to show how much power the night used. This stays on your device.

## iCloud backup (optional, off by default)

If you turn on iCloud backup in Settings, your records, and your recordings if you also choose that, are copied to your own iCloud Drive under your Apple Account. Apple stores that data under its own privacy policy. The developer cannot access it. Turning the backup off lets you keep or erase the copy in iCloud.

## Export and import

You can export all data as a zip file and share it wherever you choose. What happens to an exported file is under your control.

## Sharing

The developer does not share your data with anyone, because the developer never receives it.

## Deleting your data

You can delete a single night, delete its audio only, or erase everything in Settings. Removing the app deletes all data stored on the device. Backups in iCloud can be erased from the app's Settings or from iCloud settings on your iPhone.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Not a medical device

prosnooz is not a medical device and does not diagnose any condition. If you are worried about your sleep or breathing, talk to a doctor.

## Changes

Updates to this policy are published on this page with a new date.

## Contact

Open an issue at https://github.com/scottham/prosnooz-issues/issues

# prosnooz 隐私政策

最后更新:2026 年 11 月 15 日

prosnooz 是 prokids 开发的 iPhone 应用,用于整夜录音并分析鼾声。

- 开发者不收集你的任何数据。
- 应用没有账号、广告、统计分析,也没有第三方 SDK。
- 录音和结果只保存在你的 iPhone 上,除非你自己打开 iCloud 备份或手动导出。

**麦克风录音。** 经你授权后,应用在录制期间录音,并在本机上分析鼾声。录音不会发送给开发者,也不会上传到开发者的任何服务器。

**结果和备注。** 鼾声评分、时间线、你标记的影响因素和备注保存在本机。

**电量。** 应用在录制开始和结束时读取电量,用于显示这一晚的耗电。只保存在本机。

**iCloud 备份(可选,默认关闭)。** 打开后,记录(以及你选择备份的录音)会复制到你自己 Apple 账户下的 iCloud 云盘,由 Apple 按其隐私政策保管,开发者无法访问。

**导出与导入。** 你可以把全部数据导出为 zip 文件并自行分享,导出后的文件由你自己掌握。

**删除数据。** 你可以删除某一晚、只删除录音,或在设置中清空全部数据。删除应用会清除本机上的全部数据。iCloud 中的备份可以在应用设置或 iPhone 的 iCloud 设置中删除。

**非医疗器械。** prosnooz 不是医疗器械,不提供诊断。如对睡眠或呼吸有担心,请咨询医生。

**联系方式。** 请在 https://github.com/scottham/prosnooz-issues/issues 提交 issue。
